# Team Productivity Patterns

Scrum: sprints (2 weeks), daily standup (15min, 3 questions), sprint planning (commit), review (demo), retro (improve). Kanban: WIP limits, pull-based, continuous delivery, cycle time tracking. Shape Up (Basecamp): 6-week cycles, cool-down (2 weeks for bugs/exploration). DORA metrics: deploy frequency, lead time for change, MTTR, change failure rate. SPACE framework: Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication, Efficiency. Async-first: docs over meetings, written proposals, Loom for demos. Pair programming: complex tasks, onboarding, knowledge transfer.
