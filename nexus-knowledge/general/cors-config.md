# cors-config

CORS configuración: Access-Control-Allow-Origin: https://tudominio.com. Access-Control-Allow-Methods: GET, POST, PUT, DELETE. Access-Control-Allow-Headers: Content-Type, Authorization. Access-Control-Max-Age: 86400. Preflight: OPTIONS request. En Express: app.use(cors({origin: 'https://tudominio.com'})). En Django: django-cors-headers.
