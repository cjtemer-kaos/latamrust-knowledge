# team-communication

Team communication: 1) Standup: blockers only, not status report. 2) Retrospective: what to start/stop/continue. 3) Planning: story points, velocity. 4) Slack etiquette: threads, don't @everyone. 5) Documentation first, meeting second. 6) Decision records for ADRs. 7) Blameless post-mortems. 8) 1:1s weekly with manager.
