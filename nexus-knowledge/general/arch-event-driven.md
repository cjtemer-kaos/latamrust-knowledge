# arch-event-driven

Event-Driven Architecture: 1) Events: something happened. 2) Commands: do something. 3) Event sourcing: store events, derive state. 4) CQRS: read/write separados. 5) Sagas: orchestration de eventos. 6) Eventual consistency. Tech: Kafka, RabbitMQ, Redis Streams. Ventaja: desacoplamiento, escalabilidad.
