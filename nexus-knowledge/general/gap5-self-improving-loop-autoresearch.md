# gap5-self-improving-loop-autoresearch

SELF-IMPROVING LOOP IMPLEMENTADO: src/core/self_improving.py. SelfImprovingLoop SQLite-backed (self_improving.db). experiments table (hypothesis/approach/result/success/metrics), improvements table (area/description/impact/implemented/verified). analyze_patterns() detecta keywords en experimentos exitosos y genera auto-suggestions. daily_digest() genera reporte markdown. Inspirado en Karpathy autoresearch (brain se auto-entrena editando su propio train.py).
