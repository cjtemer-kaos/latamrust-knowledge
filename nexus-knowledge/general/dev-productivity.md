# dev-productivity

Developer productivity: 1) Pomodoro: 25min focus + 5min break. 2) Time blocking: dedicated deep work. 3) Inbox zero: process all messages. 4) GTD: capture, organize, review, do. 5) Eisenhower matrix: urgent/important. 6) Automate repetitive tasks. 7) Keyboard shortcuts mastery. 8) IDE plugins for productivity. 9) Good sleep > overtime.
