# adr-template

Architecture Decision Record (ADR): 1) Title: ADR N: [decision]. 2) Status: proposed/accepted/deprecated. 3) Context: forces at play. 4) Decision: what we decided. 5) Consequences: positive + negative. Template: ## Status: Accepted, ## Context: [situation], ## Decision: [what], ## Consequences: [outcomes]. Tools: adr-tools, markdown.
