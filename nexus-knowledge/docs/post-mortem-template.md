# post-mortem-template

Post-mortem template: 1) Summary: what happened. 2) Impact: users affected, duration. 3) Timeline: events in order. 4) Root cause: why it happened. 5) Resolution: how we fixed it. 6) Action items: prevent recurrence. 7) Lessons learned. 8) What went well. 9) What went poorly. 10) Where we got lucky. Format: blameless, factual, actionable.
