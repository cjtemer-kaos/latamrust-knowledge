# incident-management

Incident management: 1) Severity levels: SEV1 (critical, 5min), SEV2 (major, 30min), SEV3 (minor, 4h). 2) On-call rotation. 3) Escalation paths. 4) Communication templates. 5) Status page (Statuspage.io). 6) War room: dedicated channel. 7) Post-mortem: blameless, action items.
