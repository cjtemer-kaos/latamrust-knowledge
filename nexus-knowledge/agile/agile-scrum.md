# agile-scrum

Agile/Scrum: 1) Sprints de 1-2 semanas. 2) Daily standups (15 min). 3) Sprint planning y retrospective. 4) User stories con acceptance criteria. 5) Backlog grooming. 6) Definition of Done. 7) Velocity tracking. 8) Burndown charts. 9) Continuous improvement. 10) Cross-functional teams.
