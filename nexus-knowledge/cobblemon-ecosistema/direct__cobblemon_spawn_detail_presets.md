# Direct: Cobblemon spawn detail presets

Category: Cobblemon-ecosistema
Tags: direct-learned
Created: 2026-08-20T16:39:25.830223

---

Cobblemon spawn_detail_presets: los JSON en la carpeta spawn_detail_presets crean presets reutilizables para los spawn files. Un preset agrupa conditions, anticonditions y contexts bajo un nombre (nombre del archivo JSON). Ejemplo preset stronghold: condition con structures:["minecraft:stronghold"], maxY:62, neededBaseBlocks:["#minecraft:stone_bricks"], neededNearbyBlocks:["minecraft:cracked_stone_bricks","minecraft:mossy_stone_bricks"].
