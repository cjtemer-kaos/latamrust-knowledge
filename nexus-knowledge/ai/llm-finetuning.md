# llm-finetuning

Fine-tuning LLMs: 1) Full fine-tune: update all parameters (expensive). 2) LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation (efficient). 3) QLoRA: quantized LoRA. 4) Instruction tuning: follow instructions. 5) RLHF: reinforcement from human feedback. 6) DPO: direct preference optimization. 7) Dataset: instruction-input-output triples. 8) Evaluation: BLEU, ROUGE, human eval.
