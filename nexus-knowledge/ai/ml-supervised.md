# ml-supervised

Supervised learning: 1) Classification: Logistic Regression, SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Networks. 2) Regression: Linear, Ridge, Lasso, Polynomial. 3) Metrics: accuracy, precision, recall, F1, AUC-ROC, RMSE, MAE. 4) Cross-validation: k-fold. 5) Feature engineering: scaling, encoding, selection.
