Apricorns (7 colores: red, yellow, green, blue, pink, white, black) crecen en arboles apricorn. Tienen 4 etapas de crecimiento y tardan ~1 dia Minecraft en madurar; bone meal avanza 1 etapa. Al cosechar el maduro da 1 apricorn y deja un bud en etapa 1; las semillas tienen 10% de drop. Cosecha automatica: dispenser con shears activado por redstone. En composter, un apricorn tiene 65% de subir 1 nivel. Los buds/semillas se colocan sobre apricorn leaves. Crafting de Poke Balls (patron: 4 apricorn en esquinas + ingot central): Poke Ball=4 red+copper; Premier=4 white+copper; Great=red+blue+iron; Ultra=black+yellow+gold; Safari=green+white+copper; Fast=yellow+white+iron; Level=pink+red+iron; Lure=green+red+iron; Heavy=blue+black+iron; Love=pink+pink+gold; Friend=green+red+iron; Moon=black+white+iron; Sport=red+red+iron; Net=blue+white+iron; Dive=blue+blue+iron; Nest=yellow+green+iron; Repeat=red+yellow+gold; Timer=white+black+gold; Luxury=black+green+gold; Dusk=white+pink+copper; Heal=yellow+blue+gold; Quick=pink+blue+diamond; Dream=red apricorn+tumblestone+copper; Ancient Poke Ball=red apricorn+tumblestone+copper.
