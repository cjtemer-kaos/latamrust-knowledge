# Direct: Cobblemon: Evolution Stones y evolucion por piedra

Category: Cobblemon
Tags: direct-learned
Created: 2026-08-20T17:16:57.430049

---

10 piedras evolutivas: Fire, Thunder, Water, Leaf, Moon, Sun, Dawn, Dusk, Shiny, Ice. Se obtienen minando Evolution Stone Ore: Fortune da multiples, Silk Touch obliga a fundir o re-minar sin silk; la ore se funde en Furnace/Blast Furnace a 1 piedra. Crafting: 9 piedras <-> 1 Evolution Stone Block. Bloques emisores de luz (previenen spawn de mobs): Sun=5, Thunder=8, Shiny=11, Fire=13, Dawn=15. Evoluciones: Fire(Eevee->Flareon, Growlithe->Arcanine, Vulpix->Ninetales), Water(Eevee->Vaporeon, Poliwhirl->Poliwrath, Shellder->Cloyster), Thunder(Eevee->Jolteon, Pikachu->Raichu), Leaf(Eevee->Leafeon, Gloom->Vileplume), Moon(Clefairy->Clefable, Nidorina->Nidoqueen, Nidorino->Nidoking), Sun(Gloom->Bellossom, Sunkern->Sunflora), Dawn(Kirlia M->Gallade, Snorunt F->Froslass), Dusk(Murkrow->Honchkrow, Misdreavus->Mismagius, Doublade->Aegislash), Shiny(Togetic->Togekiss, Roselia->Roserade), Ice(Eevee->Glaceon, Vulpix A->Ninetales A, Crabrawler->Crabominable).
