# crypto-fundamentals

Crypto fundamentals: 1) Hashing: SHA-256 for proof of work. 2) Digital signatures: ECDSA. 3) Merkle trees: efficient verification. 4) Consensus: PoW, PoS, DPoS. 5) Smart contracts: self-executing code. 6) DeFi: lending, DEX, yield. 7) NFTs: non-fungible tokens. 8) Layer 2: rollups, state channels.
